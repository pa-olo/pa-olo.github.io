Privacy Policy

Personal Gmail Tools — last updated September 28, 2026.

  • Who uses it: only the owner of the Google account it is authorized for. It is not offered to anyone else.
  • What it accesses: the owner's Gmail messages and attachments (read-only), and the ability to create draft emails in the owner's account.
  • Where data goes: files and message details are saved only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to, sold to, or shared with any third party, and no data is used for advertising.
  • Storage and deletion: sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and local files can be deleted by the owner.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
